Adaptive Design of Immersive E-Learning Lab

The AdDIE Lab at The University of Alabama designs and studies immersive, inclusive, analytics-rich learning environments that adapt to how learners engage, collaborate, and develop knowledge.

Explore research Join the lab
Mission

Designing learning environments that see more of the learner.

AdDIE Lab conducts immersive learning research and development examining how to promote students' learning engagement and knowledge development in digital learning environments.

The work sits at the intersection of inclusive learning, immersive environments, and learning analytics, with a commitment to personalizing these environments for underrepresented learners, including learners with disabilities.
